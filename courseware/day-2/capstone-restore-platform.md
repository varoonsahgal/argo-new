## What it is

One 90-minute incident containing **seven faults**, some of which hide others. You diagnose with evidence, repair through Git and declarative configuration, prove it worked, and write down what would have caught it sooner.

**Before this:** all of Day 2, finishing at checkpoint `CP-capstone`.

## The eight modules

| # | Module | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The incident and the mental models](capstone/01-the-incident.md) | the story, the badge chain, symptom vs cause, masking |
| 2 | [🛟 Quick Rescue Guide — the Day 1 knowledge you need](capstone/02-quick-rescue-guide.md) | two clusters, Secrets, RBAC, the four gates, 401 vs 403 — **with answers** |
| 3 | [Setup and rules of engagement](capstone/03-setup-and-rules.md) | workspace, incident log, the four change paths |
| 4 | [The evidence toolbox](capstone/04-evidence-toolbox.md) | every read-only command, grouped, with where it runs |
| 5 | [The seven fault families and their hints](capstone/05-fault-families-and-hints.md) | per-family chains, hint ladders, reveal blocks |
| 6 | [Phases 1–2 — Look and Sort](capstone/06-triage.md) | your first move, the triage grid, checkpoint C1 |
| 7 | [Phases 3–5 — Fix, Prove, Learn](capstone/07-restore-verify-reflect.md) | the repair loop, verification, reflection |
| 8 | [When something goes wrong](capstone/08-troubleshooting-and-close.md) | troubleshooting, completion bars, close |

**The capstone is self-contained.** If Day 1 Lab 2 or Session 3 has gone fuzzy, module 2 re-teaches everything you need — with diagrams, examples, hints, and full answers.
